Register a public client, send the browser to `/oauth/authorize` with a PKCE challenge, and exchange the code. The access token is an RS256 JWT whose `ns` claim carries every tenant the person belongs to.

## Discovery

```sh
curl -s https://auth.latchkey.id/.well-known/openid-configuration | jq .
```

## A single-page app

1. Generate a verifier and its S256 challenge.
2. Redirect to `authorize` with `response_type=code`, your `client_id`, `redirect_uri`, `code_challenge` and a `state`.
3. On the callback, POST `code` + `code_verifier` to `/oauth/token`.

> Latchkey signs people in by email link, which opens in a **new tab** — keep the verifier in `localStorage`, not `sessionStorage`.

## Verifying in Go

| Claim | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `sub` | the identity: uuid5 of the credential |
| `ns` | namespace → level, scoped to your client's org |
| `aud` | always `latchkey` for access tokens |
