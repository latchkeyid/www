Latchkey never calls back into your product during sign-in. Tenancy is resolved by your gateway from its own read models per request — the `myAccess` pattern — so a hook is a policy in your service, not a webhook you must keep up.

## What you can react to

- **Membership changes** — signed webhooks (`WebhookEndpoint`), retried, dead-lettered.
- **Refused sign-ins** — the attempts dashboard, or `attemptStats` over the gateway.
- **New devices** — device attestation results ride the token's `amr`.
